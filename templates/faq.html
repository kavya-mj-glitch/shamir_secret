Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ShardSafe, encryption, and key management.

Shamir's Secret Sharing is a cryptographic algorithm created by Adi Shamir. It divides a secret (in our case, an AES encryption key) into multiple parts called "shares." You configure a threshold K out of N total shares. Any K shares can reconstruct the original secret, but fewer than K shares reveal absolutely nothing about it.
AES-256-GCM is considered military-grade encryption. It uses a 256-bit key, making brute-force attacks computationally infeasible. The GCM (Galois/Counter Mode) provides authenticated encryption, meaning it not only encrypts the data but also verifies its integrity during decryption. If anyone tampers with the encrypted file, decryption will fail.
